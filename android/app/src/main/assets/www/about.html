About Paycheck

Paycheck is an independent paycheck estimation tool designed to help users estimate take-home pay, taxes, overtime earnings, and payroll deductions.

The calculations provided by this app are estimates only and should not be considered financial, tax, legal, or payroll advice.

Results may differ from actual paycheck amounts depending on employer payroll systems, tax elections, credits, deductions, exemptions, and individual circumstances.

Always consult official government resources, a tax professional, or your payroll department before making financial decisions. Those sources are listed on the Government Sources page.

What you can enter

You enter a wage, the hours on one paycheck, and any overtime. Filing status, state, a 401(k) percent, health premiums, extra withholding, and a local wage tax can all be included. The result estimates that check, and a year made of checks like it, from publicly available tax information. It is not an official government calculation.

Who publishes it

Paycheck is published by MadoTrak in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. It is not affiliated with any government agency. Questions go through the contact page. How the site handles information is in the privacy policy.
