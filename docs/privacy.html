Privacy

Privacy policy

Updated October 20, 2026.

This policy covers the paycheck calculator published by Mado Applications. The calculator estimates take-home pay. It does not ask you to create an account.

What you type

Wage, hours, filing status, state, and any deductions you enter are used in your browser to calculate the estimate. Those entries are saved in local storage on that browser so the form can reopen with the same numbers. They are not sent to a Takehome account, because there is no account. Clearing the site data, or using a private window that discards storage when it closes, removes them.

What we do not collect

The calculator does not ask for your name, Social Security number, bank account, employer, or address. Do not type those into the wage or notes fields. There is no sale of personal information.

Advertising

Pages include a Google AdSense units. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve and measure ads, including ads based on your visits to this and other sites.

Some articles also include affiliate links, which are labeled. If you use one, the publisher may earn a commission.

You can read how Google uses data from sites that use its services at Google’s advertising policy. Ad personalization can be changed at Google ad settings. You can also block third-party cookies in your browser.

Children

The calculator is written for adults looking at wages. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect their information.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page will change with it. Questions about privacy can be sent to Samad@samadrobinson.com.
